## European Society of Medicine

The European Society of Medicine (ESMED) is an international medical society. It publishes the peer-reviewed journal [Medical Research Archives](https://esmed.org/medical-research-archives/), and its [Global Health Center](https://esmed.org/global-health-center/) and advisory committees publish policy frameworks, outbreak reports, responses to public consultations and stories.

### New: Madagascar's Long Hunger

[![Madagascar's Long Hunger: How a century of land loss turned drought into famine](https://esmed.org/stories/madagascars-long-hunger/img/share.jpg)](https://esmed.org/stories/madagascars-long-hunger/)

An interactive story from the [Global Nutrition Alliance](https://esmed.org/global-nutrition-alliance/) on how a century of land loss turned drought into famine in the far south of Madagascar. It follows the town of Ifotaka from the 1920s to today, with maps, photographs and seven years of satellite imagery.
